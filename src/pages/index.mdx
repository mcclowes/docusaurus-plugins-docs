# Docusaurus plugins

A family of small, focused plugins for Docusaurus v3 — documented and **dogfooded** on this site. Every plugin you read about here is installed and running right now on the site you're looking at.

## Plugins

- **[Banner](/docs/banner/overview)** — dismissable announcement at the top of the page
- **[Cookie consent](/docs/cookie-consent/overview)** — GDPR modal with Google Consent Mode v2
- **[Glossary](/docs/glossary/overview)** — auto-generated glossary, inline term tooltips, auto-linking
- **[Marginalia](/docs/marginalia/overview)** — editorial sidenotes that pack top-down in the right margin
- **[New post toast](/docs/new-post-toast/overview)** — toast notifications for new blog posts since your last visit
- **[omg](/docs/omg/overview)** — build-time OMG → OpenAPI 3.1 compiler
- **[Starter](/docs/starter/overview)** — template for building your own Docusaurus plugin
- **[Statuspage](/docs/statuspage/overview)** — incident banner from any public Statuspage.io
